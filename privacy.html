Privacy Policy

Sector Chakra app and boards
Nifty 50 F&O Sectors RRGBeta

Effective 3 October 2026

This policy covers the Sector Chakra Android app and the web boards it displays (Nifty 50, F&O Sectors, RRG, Rollover, PCR and 44 EMA). The short version: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect any personal information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not use advertising IDs, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools. The developer receives no data about you or how you use the app.

What happens when the app loads a board

The app shows web pages, so loading a board makes ordinary internet requests to two services:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts the boards and their market data.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC), which supplies the typefaces.

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic device and browser details (the app identifies itself as “SectorChakraApp”), and they may keep standard server logs under their own policies: GitHub Privacy Statement, Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive or have access to those logs.

Stored on your phone

Android’s web view keeps a normal cache of the pages so they load faster. It stays on your device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app removes it. The boards set no cookies of their own.

Links to other sites

Tapping a stock opens its page on the National Stock Exchange of India website in your browser. That site has its own privacy policy, which applies once you are there.

Paid features

A paid “Pro” tier is planned but does not exist yet. Nothing can be bought in the app today. If Pro ever needs sign-in or payment details, this policy will be updated to say exactly what is collected and why before any such information is collected, and payments would be handled by Google Play, not by the developer.

Children

The app is a market-data tool meant for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Market data

Market data comes from public sources and may be delayed or inaccurate. The app is for information only. It is not investment advice, and not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
